Valiant Faith

Real prayers. Shared hope. A living picture of faith.

Come as you are

Stand together in prayer.

You do not need polished words to come before God. Bring your burdens, your gratitude, and your hope.

Visit Valiant Faith

Valiant Faith is a prayer initiative of Faith Based Pilot, inviting people to turn to God and lift one another up in prayer. It is a place to share what is on your heart, pause with someone else’s prayer, and remember that faith is lived in community.

At the heart of the initiative is the Prayer Wall: living artworks formed from the words of real, approved public prayers. The prayers themselves make the picture. As more prayers are added, the letters become smaller and the image grows more detailed.

Look closer and the picture becomes words you can read. Each prayer is more than part of an artwork: it is an invitation to stop, care, and pray for another person.

Take part

Share. Read. Pray.

There is room for your prayer, and there is someone you can pray for.

Share a prayer

Offer a prayer, a request, or words of thanksgiving through Valiant Faith. Submissions are reviewed before appearing publicly. Only submit words you are comfortable sharing with others, and avoid including sensitive personal details.

Explore the Prayer Wall

See artworks built from prayer. Zoom in to read the words, highlight a prayer, and open it to read in full. Every contribution becomes part of something shared.

Pray for someone

Take a moment to read another person’s prayer and bring it before God. You can participate simply by being present and praying.

Invite others

Share Valiant Faith with your family, friends, or church community. Invite them to add their prayers and join you in praying for others.

A personal invitation

Need someone to pray for you?

Some requests are not meant for a public wall. If you would like to ask for prayer privately, email Faith Based Pilot directly. Requests sent by email will not be published on the Prayer Wall.
